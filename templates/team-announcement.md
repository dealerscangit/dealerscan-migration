# Team Announcement Template
**When to send:** Phase 8 — after v3.10 is approved by Web Store and you've tested it on a real Tekion deal yourself.
**Channel:** Whatever you normally use to talk to your colleagues (group text, dealership Slack/Teams, in-person at morning meeting, whatever's normal — don't make this feel formal)
**Tone:** Casual, confident, brief. They don't need the technical story.

---

## VERSION A — If migration was clean (most customer data preserved)

Hey team — DealerScan is back online and running on a brand new backend. Few things to know:

1. **Update Chrome.** The extension auto-updated overnight. Open Chrome, go to chrome://extensions, hit the refresh button at the top right if you don't see DealerScan. You should be on v3.10.

2. **Sign in again.** First time you open DealerScan, it'll ask you to sign in to Google again — that's normal. Use your usual Google account. Takes 10 seconds.

3. **Re-enter your name.** The Settings will be empty for the first time. Pop it open, type your first name, save. Done.

4. **Everything else works the same.** Your customer folders are preserved. The scan shortcut on your phone still works. Upload to Tekion still works.

If anything looks weird, screenshot it and send it to me. I'm watching the logs.

— Brandon

## What NOT to put in this message
- ❌ Don't mention the suspension by name
- ❌ Don't mention "compliance" — that scares people unnecessarily
- ❌ Don't mention "GLBA" or "legal"
- ❌ Don't apologize excessively — confidence is contagious, anxiety is too
- ❌ Don't promise no future downtime — over-promising is a trap

## What TO emphasize
- ✅ The system is back and stable
- ✅ The change to their workflow is small (sign in + re-enter name)
- ✅ They have a direct line to you if something breaks
- ✅ You're proactive (mention you're watching the logs)
