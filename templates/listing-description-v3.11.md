DealerScan is a productivity tool for car salespeople using Tekion DMS. It bridges the gap between scanning customer documents (driver's license, insurance, credit application, etc.) on your phone and getting them into the Tekion deal jacket — without the manual download-and-reupload that usually slows down deals.

How it works:
1. Scan customer documents using a paired iOS Shortcut (set up once)
2. Documents land in a shared Google Drive™ folder, named for the customer
3. Open the Tekion deal jacket in Chrome, click the DealerScan icon
4. Select the customer folder, choose docs, click upload — files are injected directly into the deal jacket attachments

Built by a salesperson for salespeople. No subscriptions, no data collection beyond what's needed to run, no ads, no third-party data sharing.

Required permissions:
• Google Drive™ — read and write your dealership's customer document folders
• Tekion (app.tekioncloud.com) — inject documents into deal jackets
• Apps Script (script.google.com) — communicate with the dealership's own backend

DealerScan is currently used at a single dealership and is not yet sold commercially.
